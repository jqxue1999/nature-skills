# The user's own plotting habits (always on)

These rules apply to every data figure for this user. Where they conflict with the defaults in `stance.md`, these
rules win.

## Look

- Left and bottom spines only. No text may cover an axis line.
- One sans-serif font in every figure of the paper, at sizes that match each other.
- Our method stands out (solid, stronger, drawn on top). Baselines are quieter.
- One muted palette for the whole paper, and the same colours in the LaTeX text.

## Layout

- Compact, with little white space. Keep the figure's size in the paper and trim its margins.
- Panels side by side line up. A legend sits where it obviously belongs to the panels it describes.

## Consistency

- When some figures are redrawn, redraw all of them, so the whole paper has one style.

## Tables or charts

- Do not force a small table into a chart. Use a chart only when the trend is the message.
